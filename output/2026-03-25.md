# Concepts of the Day

## Diffusion Models

Diffusion models are a type of generative model that use a process called diffusion to generate samples from a probability distribution. The diffusion process involves iteratively adding noise to the input data until it becomes indistinguishable from random noise.

**Example:** A concrete example of diffusion models is the use of diffusion-based image generation models, which can generate high-quality images by iteratively refining the input noise.

**Why it matters:** Diffusion models matter because they provide a powerful tool for generating high-quality samples from complex probability distributions. They have been used in a wide range of applications, including image and video generation, music synthesis, and data augmentation.

**In today's research:** Diffusion models are connected to today's papers through the use of diffusion-based optical flow estimation in the DA-Flow paper, and the use of diffusion models for generative ARPG in the WildWorld paper.
